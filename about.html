About The Conductors

Project description

This group of three was in charge of conducting the data analysis. We are all involved in sports (with some in intramural teams), and found a frustration of dealing with forfeits in past intramural games. Our mission was to utilize the material we learned in class to investigate an underlying problem within Northeastern's recreational life.

We would love your feedback! Feel free to contact us at intramuralinvestigators@northeastern.edu to help out.
