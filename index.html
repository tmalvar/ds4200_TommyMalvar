A Dive into the Forfeits of Northeastern Intramurals

Overall Introduction

Intramural sports are fun ways to connect with peers, destress from classes or work, and try different activities.

However, these activities are noncommittal, which presents opportunities for people to miss events. When too many players on a team miss a game, the opposition wins by a forfeit. This inconvenience affects all players and officials, as their time and effort was wasted for each canceled event.

In addition, a penalty fee is charged against the forfeiting team, which hurts captains financially and may fester animosity within teams. By examining data that documents every match outcome, managers and players can prepare for forfeits in the future. In this project, we will investigate Northeastern’s intramural activities to understand what factors cause forfeiting and how they affect teams’ success.

To learn more about the forfeit issue or general intramural information at Northeastern, you can visit the Northeastern Recreation Website or register for a Sport by visiting the IM League University Page.

Introduction to the Data

For our data analysis, we utilized a dataset called "Intramural_Clean.csv". This cleaned file contains relevant information for almost every logged intramural game in the Fall of 2025. We included games that occurred at one of the five main locations at the Boston campus. There are over 700 rows of documented games, with each containing information for the following variables:

Variable Name Details
Date Date of game played
Time Start Time of game
AM/PM Half of day game was played
Time_Cleaned Formatted version of Time column
Facility Location of game played
Latitude Latitude coordinate of location
Longitude Longitude cooridnate of location
Location_Type Either an indoor or outdoor location
Game_Time_Category Either an "Early" or "Late" game, as determined by the study conductors.
Sport Sport of the game played
Forfeit Forfeit status of games (either "Yes" or "No")

Most of the variables were initially included. Data such as forfeit status and game time category were added by cross referencing with the initial website, as they were not included. Excluded games consisted of any that were:

  • Not played in Boston (ie. Oakland)
  • Did not have an exact location (ie. ESports)
  • Were not played yet (null values)

The dataset helps investigate different variables to determine their influences on forfeits. The following questions were identified and investigated through data analysis:

  • "Did the type of sport have an influence on forfeit rates?"
  • "Did location have an influence on forfeit rates?"
  • "Are there groups / people who forfeited multiple times?"
  • "Did the time of game influence forfeit rates?"

Summary & Contact

In this visualization analysis, we try to investigate the forfeit issue for Northeastern intramural sports. We looked at different factors that influence the issue to help mitigate outlying flaws in the system.

This data only covers the Fall 2025 semester and does not document games from previous semesters, which could determine if these patterns are seasonal.
