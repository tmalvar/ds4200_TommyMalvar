Does Time Influence Forfeits?

This visualization shows the relationship between game start time and whether a game was forfeited.

According to the chart, most forfeits occur after 6 PM. However, the overall box sizes between the “Forfeit” and “No Forfeit” groups are similar, suggesting time plays a minor role.
